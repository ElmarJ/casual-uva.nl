**Structural  teaching deserves permanent contracts**

Join Casual UvA’s demonstration for secure employment for lecturers.

Nov 25 2026 · 12:00-14:00 · REC grassfield

 

D4 lecturers bring expertise and dedication to education. They care about students, their intellectual interests and their futures. That commitment deserves secure employment, not precarity. Yet for D4 lecturers, employment ends after four years, even when the work continues. Instead of retaining teaching expertise, the university keeps replacing the people who provide it. New temporary lecturers take over work that never went away.

 

For students, this means losing teachers who know their courses, support their learning and supervise their work. For staff, it means saying goodbye to committed colleagues and losing teaching expertise that departments have helped develop. Repeated recruitment and onboarding also add to permanent staff’s workload- leaving less time for other teaching and research activities.

 

D4 lecturers are passionate about education. They deserve the opportunity to build a future in teaching. Keeping them benefits students, colleagues and the university as a whole. Valuing education means valuing the people who deliver it. Casual UvA calls on the university to recognize structural teaching work and give the lecturers doing it permanent contracts.

 

Students, staff and everyone who supports fair employment at the UvA are welcome. Stand with your lecturers and colleagues. Join us and bring someone along.

Want to help organise or distribute flyers? Contact us at [ask.casualuva@gmail.com](mailto:ask.casualuva@gmail.com "mailto\:ask.casualuva@gmail.com")
